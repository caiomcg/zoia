### New

- **Entrance sounds.** Pick a sound from Myinstants in **Settings › Sounds › Entrance sound**
  and it plays for everyone in the channel when you arrive, right after the usual join sound.
  It plays every time you join a channel, moving between channels included, and you hear it
  too. Use the play button beside any result to preview it first.
- **Your own say over everyone else's.** In the same card you can mute everyone's entrance
  sounds or set their volume. To mute just one person's, open the menu on their name and tick
  **Mute their entrance sound**. This is separate from muting their sound effects: each one
  leaves the other alone.

### Good to know

- An entrance sound only plays if you arrive with your microphone on, and never in the away
  channel. Reconnecting after a dropped connection doesn't play it again.
- Each sound plays for 5 seconds at most and fades out. Someone's entrance sound plays at most
  once a minute for you, however often they hop between channels.
- The sound you choose is kept on that computer, not on your account: on another computer,
  choose it again. A new sound is used from the next channel you join.
- Windows and Mac. Android doesn't play entrance sounds, and people on older versions of Zoia
  won't hear yours.

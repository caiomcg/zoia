---
summary: Cameras get tiles of their own, and microphones start muted.
---

### New

- **Open a camera from its button.** While someone's camera is on, a camera button sits at the
  end of their row in the channel list. Click it to put their camera on the stage, and again to
  take it off. With the list collapsed, hover their avatar: the button is in the card, under
  their picture. Clicking someone's row still opens only their screen.
- **Android:** a bar above the grid lists everyone with a camera on. Tap someone to put their
  camera on the grid as a tile of its own, and tap again to take it off.

### Improved

- **Everything on the stage at once.** Choosing someone's screen no longer takes your own off
  the stage. Your screen, your camera, the screens you chose and the cameras you put on all
  show together. In the mosaic, click a tile to enlarge it and click the enlarged one to bring
  the mosaic back. The spotlight shows the first of them large until you click another.
- **Cameras are tiles of their own** instead of sitting over the corner of a screen, where
  they covered what was being shared. This applies on Windows, Mac and Android.
- **Your own screen stays on your stage** with the preview off, with the eye button to turn
  the preview back on.
- The collapsed channel list has larger avatars with more room between them.

### Fixed

- **Windows:** blank "League of Legends" windows no longer pile up on the taskbar and in the
  source picker while listing sources or broadcasting League.
- Your own screen preview no longer draws your camera over it. Viewers still see your camera.

### Good to know

- **Microphones start muted, on both ends.** The camera dialog's mute box is ticked every time
  you open it; unmute there or from the player. As a viewer you hear nobody's microphone until
  you turn it on from their tile. Neither choice is remembered, so expect to unmute each time.
- Nobody's camera opens by itself any more: look for the camera button on their row.
- **Android:** one person is still heard at a time. Their microphone starts off; turn it on with
  the button on their tile.

### Fixed

- **A shared window that changes size now changes the stream's size with it** on an NVIDIA
  graphics card. A browser shared in a window and then made fullscreen used to stay at the
  window's first shape, smaller than the chosen quality and with the fullscreen picture shrunk
  inside it. It now fills the chosen quality, and a window snapped to half the screen goes
  out at that shape, with no black bars. The change follows a moment after the window stops
  changing size.

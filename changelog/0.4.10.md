### Fixed

- **Closing the window you are sharing ends the broadcast** on Windows. Before, viewers could be left
  watching its last picture with the broadcast still shown as live.
- **Closing a shared browser window no longer moves the broadcast to another one** you
  already had open. A launcher handing over to its game, such as League of Legends, still
  carries the broadcast across.
- **The broadcast ends sooner** after the shared window closes: in about a second and a
  half instead of around five.

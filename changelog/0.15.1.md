---
summary: Enhanced noise suppression no longer cuts your voice, and a tidier sidebar.
---

### Fixed

- **Enhanced noise suppression no longer cuts your voice.** Since 0.14.7, with Settings ›
  Voice › Noise suppression on **Enhanced**, much of what you said reached others as silence,
  so your voice kept dropping out and coming back. It sounds as it did in 0.14.6 again.
- **Sound that stops when your output device changes starts again by itself.**

### Improved

- **Text chats are back on the Channels tab**, under the voice channels. The **Chats** tab now
  holds your direct messages and the people on the server, and its count is for direct messages
  only.
- **Double-click someone in a voice channel** to send them a direct message. It works on the
  small faces under other channels too.
- **Notifications are at the top right** of the window, where the bell is visible whatever the
  sidebar shows. Its list opens downward.
- **The settings gear is back** beside your microphone and headphones. Settings is still in the
  menu that opens from your name, too.

summary: GIFs in every chat, hear yourself, copy images and messages, and notification sound controls.
---

### New

- **GIFs in every chat.** A **GIF** button beside the emoji button opens trending GIFs from
  KLIPY. Type to search, scroll for more, and click one to send it. GIFs play right in the
  chat, in text chats, direct messages and threads alike.
- **Hear yourself.** Settings › Voice › Input has a **Hear yourself** switch that plays your
  microphone back to you on both ears, exactly as others hear it, with noise suppression,
  volume and sensitivity applied. While it is on you are deafened: nobody hears you and you
  hear nobody. Turning it off, or closing Settings, puts you back as you were. Use headphones.
- **Copy a message or an image.** Right-click a message to copy its text, or the picture
  itself if it has one. The photo viewer has a **Copy image** button too.

### Improved

- **Notification sounds have their own controls.** Settings › Notifications › Sounds can mute
  the event sound and the mention and direct message sound, and set the volume of each.
- **No text cursor over people.** Hovering or dragging over someone in the channel list, the
  people list or a person's menu no longer shows the text cursor or selects their name.
- **No pulsing green dot on the share buttons** while you stream. The buttons still turn green,
  and the live card still says what you share.

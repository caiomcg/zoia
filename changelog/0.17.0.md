---
summary: Errors and warnings move to the bell, chat tabs you can pin, reorder and cycle, one device at a time, and admins can rename people.
---

### New

- **Admins can rename people.** Settings › Accounts has a **Rename** button beside each
  person. Everyone sees the new name at once, that person included.
- **Pinned chat tabs stay where you put them.** Pins are remembered per account when you close
  and reopen Zoia. Drag a tab to move it: pinned tabs reorder among pinned ones, the rest among
  the rest.
- **Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab** go to the next and previous tab, the channel included while
  you are in one, wrapping around at the ends.
- **Who wrote to you, at a glance.** Someone with unread direct messages shows a count beside
  their name in the channel list, and a pinned or open direct message tab shows their picture.

### Improved

- **Errors and warnings go to the bell, not banners across the window.** Errors are red and
  warnings amber, and the same problem happening again adds to its entry instead of piling up.
  Clicking the "run as administrator" warning restarts Zoia normally, as its banner's button did.
- **One device at a time in a channel.** Joining a channel on another device takes your other
  devices out of theirs. They stay signed in and say why they left, so you can join again there.
- **Chat tabs cycle.** Opening a chat replaces the one unpinned tab instead of adding another;
  pinned tabs stay.
- **Direct messages no longer go to the bell.** Their count is on the **Chats** tab instead,
  without the "@" beside it.
- **Everyone in a channel is listed**, one below the other, even when you are not in it.
- **Chats open at the latest message**, and following a reply's notification opens its thread.
- **Joining an event marks you going.** The event card opens its details: while the event is on
  it leads with **Join**, with **Maybe** and **Can't go** under it; before it starts, it asks
  whether you are going. Who can't go is listed too, below who is coming.
- **Android:** volume controls and audio gain limits, and a reworked voice screen.

### Fixed

- **Broadcasts no longer turn soft when someone opens them late.** On NVIDIA and AMD graphics,
  a broadcast nobody was watching yet could drop its bitrate to the minimum, and whoever opened
  it got a blurry picture until it was shared again.

### Good to know

- Errors and warnings appear only inside Zoia by default. To also get them as system
  notifications, turn on **Something goes wrong in Zoia** in Settings › Notifications.

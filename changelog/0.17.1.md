### Fixed

- **No microphone no longer keeps you out of voice.** Without a microphone, or with one that
  cannot be opened, you still join the channel's voice: you hear everyone and can watch every
  stream. The microphone button shows it is off and opens the list of microphones; plug one in
  or choose one and you are heard straight away.
- **No speakers or headphones is fine too.** You join and watch the streams as usual, and sound
  plays as soon as an output is plugged in.

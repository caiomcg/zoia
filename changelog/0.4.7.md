### Fixed

- **Tile controls stay on one line** when you drag a side-by-side broadcast narrower in the
  mosaic. The volume, fullscreen and close buttons used to wrap onto a second row.
- **Your close button sits beside fullscreen** on your own tile, at the bottom like on the
  other tiles, instead of under the mosaic switch.

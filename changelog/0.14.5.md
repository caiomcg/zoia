### New

- **Chats in tabs.** Each chat you open from the sidebar gets its own tab beside the stage,
  so you can keep several open and switch between them. Close one with its **×** or with the
  middle mouse button. Each chat keeps what you were typing until you send it, even when you
  switch tabs.
- **Pinned tabs.** Right-click a chat's tab and choose **Pin zaba** to keep it at the front
  of the tab row. A pinned tab shows a pin icon.
- **A menu on messages.** Right-click a message to reply to it or delete it. Right-click a
  link in a message to open it or copy it.
- **Amon** joins the credits in **Settings › About**, for chats in tabs.

### Good to know

- Tabs, and which ones are pinned, last until you close Zoia. The next time you open it,
  you start with one chat again.
- With every tab closed and no channel joined, pick a chat in the sidebar to open it.
- Windows and Mac.

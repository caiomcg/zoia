### New

- **A livelier Zoia.** Short, quiet animations throughout:
  - Dialogs and the language menu open and close smoothly instead of popping.
  - People slide into the channel list when they join and out when they leave, in step with
    the join and leave sounds, and an avatar gives a small pulse when that person goes live.
  - New broadcasts fade in on the stage and in the thumbnail strip; one that is still on its
    way shimmers until its picture arrives.
  - Messages at the top drop in and lift away when you dismiss them, and the connection
    details fade in when you hover the status light.
  - The highlight glides to the channel you pick.
- **About credits Victor,** who brought Zoia to the Mac, and describes Nycholas's AMD hardware
  encoding work. Everyone's picture there is their GitHub avatar.

### Improved

- **Switching channels feels like a move, not a reconnect.** No more "Connecting…", flashing
  status light or dimmed buttons while you hop, and the channel you land in lists who is there
  straight away.
- **The sidebar collapses and opens instantly,** so the rest of the window no longer slides
  and shifts along with it.

### Good to know

- If your system is set to reduce motion (Windows: Settings › Accessibility › Visual effects ›
  Animation effects; macOS: Accessibility › Display › Reduce motion), Zoia keeps still.
- The pictures in About are loaded from GitHub when you open it; offline, they show initials.

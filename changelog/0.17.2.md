---
summary: Deafen keeps the streams playing, idle moves you to the away channel, and a fix for black browser videos.
---

### New

- **Fix for browser videos that show up black (Windows).** When you pick a screen to share,
  the share picker now checks whether Chrome, Edge or Firefox is open without the video fix.
  If it is, it says so and offers to restart that browser with the fix, tabs restored. Without
  the fix, viewers can see a black box where a video plays. You can undo it from the same
  place.
- **Read a notification in full from the bell.** A notification that doesn't lead anywhere
  now opens in full in the bell's list, with **Copy** and **Back**. Escape goes back before it
  closes the list. Mentions and events still take you to them.

### Improved

- **Deafen silences the voice chat, not the streams.** Deafened, you hear no voices,
  soundboard or entrance sounds and no sound cues, but the broadcasts you watch keep playing.
  Each broadcast still has its own mute.
- **Only time away from your computer counts as idle.** Sitting in a channel while you play
  or work, just listening, no longer gets you disconnected. You count as idle only after a
  while with no keyboard or mouse, or with the screen locked. On a server with an away
  channel, you are moved there instead and Zoia tells you why; pick any channel to go back.

### Good to know

- The browser fix closes the browser and reopens it. For Chrome and Edge it also adds the
  setting to the browser's taskbar, Start menu and desktop shortcuts, so it stays on next
  time.

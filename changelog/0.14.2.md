---
summary: Updating Zoia from Program Files no longer restarts it over and over.
---

### Fixed

- **Zoia could restart every two minutes while updating.** On a Zoia installed for everyone,
  in Program Files, an update needs administrator rights. If the Windows prompt asking for them
  went unanswered, Zoia reopened on the old version and, with automatic updates on, tried the
  same update again, over and over. Zoia now waits for your answer, reopens straight away if you
  say no, and asks before trying an update that didn't go through.

### Good to know

- Windows only, and only for Zoia installed for everyone on the computer.

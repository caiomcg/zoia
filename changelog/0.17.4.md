summary: A calendar of the month with every birthday, a peek into other channels, birthday greetings and shorter Zumbido sounds.
---

### New

- **A calendar of the month.** The **Calendar** button in the Events panel's heading opens
  the month as a grid, with the day you pick listed beside it: its birthdays, then its
  events. Click an event to open it, or pick a day from today on and **Schedule on this day**.
  Repeating events show their later occurrences too, lighter and dashed. The arrow keys and
  Page Up/Down move through days and months; **Today** comes back.
- **Every birthday is on the calendar, all year.** You can see birthdays coming, not only on
  the day.
- **Wish someone a happy birthday from the bell.** Someone else's birthday notification
  offers a few greetings; pick one and it goes to them in a direct message. Once sent, the
  notification says so.
- **See into a channel without joining it.** The channel list now shows who in another
  channel is muted or deafened, with the same marks as inside it, and what someone live there
  is sharing. Hover a live person to see a recent picture of their share.
- **Cut a Zumbido sound short.** The Zumbido panel has a **Length** slider under the volume:
  everything you play from the panel or a shortcut stops there for everyone. A pad can keep
  a cut of its own when you edit it, with a preview.
- **Cut every sound effect for yourself.** **Settings › Sounds › Zumbido › Cut sound effects
  after** stops every effect you hear at that length, from 1 to 10 seconds. Everyone else
  still hears each sound as long as whoever played it chose.

### Fixed

- **Muting your microphone or leaving voice now stops the Zumbido sound you were playing**,
  for you and for everyone hearing it.

### Improved

- **Myinstants search results come in pages** in the Zumbido panel, with previous and next
  buttons, instead of a **Load more** button.

### Good to know

- While you share, Zoia sends a small picture of your share every 15 seconds so people in
  other channels can see what is on. It can be up to 15 seconds old, and someone looking at
  it is not counted among who is watching. With a camera background on, the picture shows
  the background, as viewers see it.
- Someone still on an older Zoia sends no picture: their broadcast shows "No picture yet".
- People on an older Zoia hear your Zumbido sounds whole, up to 10 seconds, until they
  update.
- The birthday greetings are the same few words in every language.

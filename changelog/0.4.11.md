### New

- **Picture-in-picture.** The new button next to fullscreen on a broadcast you are watching
  opens it in a small window that stays on top of your other windows, even with Zoia
  minimized. Drag its top edge to move it and its edges to resize it; hover over it for
  mute, volume and close.
- While a broadcast is in picture-in-picture, its place on the stage says so, and
  **Bring back** returns it. Its sound and volume work from either place.

### Good to know

- One broadcast can be in picture-in-picture at a time; choosing another moves the window
  to it.
- The window closes when you stop watching that broadcast or close Zoia.

---
summary: React to chat messages with an emoji, and hear when someone @mentions you.
---

### New

- **Reactions.** Hover a message and click the smiley beside **Reply** to react with an
  emoji. Reactions show under the message with a count, yours highlighted; click one to add
  yours or take it back, and hover it to see who reacted. Everyone sees them at once.
- **Mention alerts.** A chat with unread messages that @mention you gets an amber **@**
  beside its unread count, in the sidebar and on the chat tab. It clears once you read the
  chat.
- **A sound for mentions:** a bell of its own, unlike the channel sounds, when someone
  @mentions you and the chat is not on your screen. Turn it off in **Settings → Sounds**.
- **Each sound has its own volume** in **Settings → Sounds**, on top of the overall volume.

### Improved

- Being disconnected from a channel, by an admin or for sitting idle, is now said in yellow
  rather than red, and the message goes away by itself after a minute.
- You are now disconnected from a channel after thirty minutes neither sharing nor watching,
  instead of ten.

### Fixed

- Long release notes, like these, scroll instead of being cut off at the bottom.

### Good to know

- The mention sound is on by default. Your sounds keep their settings, each at full volume
  until you change it.

---
summary: Set your microphone's volume, and boost one that is too quiet.
---

### New

- **Input volume.** Settings → Voice → Input has a slider for how loud your microphone is
  sent, from 0 to 100%. The level meter below it moves with it, so you can see the change as
  you speak.
- **Boost.** If your microphone is still too quiet at 100%, click **Boost** next to the
  volume. A second slider appears that takes it up to 300%. Click Boost again to turn it
  off; the level you set is kept for next time.
- Both apply right away, even while you are in voice. Double-click a slider to reset it, or
  use the mouse wheel to adjust it a step at a time.

### Fixed

- After being removed from a channel and joining one again, the People list no longer shows
  the previous channel's people for a moment.

### Good to know

- A boosted microphone also picks up more of the room. Loud peaks are softened rather than
  cut off, but if you sound distorted, lower the boost a little.
- If Boost is not enough, check the microphone's own level in your system's sound settings.

### New

- **Accounts.** Zoia now signs you in with an email and a password instead of an invite file.
  On the sign-in screen, enter your server's address (no need for `https://`) and either sign
  in or choose **Create account**. A new account waits for an admin to approve it, and the app
  moves on by itself once they do. Your password is asked for once per computer or device.
- **Admins.** Admins approve or reject new accounts, disable someone, and give a forgotten
  password a one-time reset code, all from **Settings → Accounts**. A red number on the
  settings button shows how many people are waiting. The super admin also makes and removes
  admins.
- **Crowns.** Admins wear a small silver crown on their picture, and the super admin a gold
  one, so everyone can see who looks after the server.
- **Your account.** **Settings → Profile** shows the email you sign in with, lets you change
  your password, and lists the machines signed in as you, so you can sign one out.
- **Forgot your password?** Ask an admin for a code, then choose **Have a code from an
  admin?** on the sign-in screen.
- **Android.** The Android app signs in the same way: the server's address, then your
  account.

### Improved

- Your name and picture belong to you, so they are the same on every machine you use.
- Someone who is disabled, or signed out from another machine, leaves the channel within
  seconds.

### Good to know

- **Everyone creates an account once.** Invite files no longer work, and machines set up with
  one are signed out.
- Passwords need at least 6 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number
  and a symbol. The sign-in screen ticks each one off as you type.
- Only admins can add, rename or remove channels. Everyone can still join any of them.

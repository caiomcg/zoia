### New

- **Direct messages.** Write to one person privately: click someone under **People** in the
  **Chats** tab, or use the **+** over **Direct messages**. Only the two of you can read the
  conversation, its photos and its replies; admins and bots cannot. A direct message plays the
  mention sound.
- **Threads.** Point at a message and choose **Reply in thread** (or right-click it). The thread
  opens beside the chat, and the message shows how many replies it has and when the last came.
  Deleting the message deletes its thread.
- **See who is typing.** "Ana is typing…" shows under the message box, in chats, direct
  messages and threads.
- **Scheduled events.** Plan a game night with a title, a start and an end, a channel and an
  optional description, and say whether you are going, maybe, or can't go.
  - Schedule one from the calendar button beside the sidebar tabs, or right-click a channel and
    choose **Schedule event here**.
  - Events can repeat daily, weekly or monthly.
  - From an hour before an event starts until it ends, it shows at the top of the sidebar.
    Several move along on their own; drag them or use the mouse wheel to move through them.
    Point at one for its details, your answer and the way in; click it to join its channel.
    The **×** hides it here, and it stays in the Events panel.
- **A notification bell** in your bar at the bottom of the sidebar. It counts what is new: an
  event you are going to starting, a direct message, an @mention. Messages from the same person
  are grouped, and clicking one takes you to the chat or into the event's channel. Notifications
  are kept for a day.
- **System notifications** for the same things, while Zoia is not the window in front. Each
  kind can be turned off, in the bell and in the system separately, in
  **Settings › Notifications**.
- **An event sound.** "An event you are going to starts" has its own sound in
  **Settings › Sounds**.

### Improved

- **The sidebar has two tabs, Channels and Chats.** Channels is voice; Chats holds the text
  chats, direct messages and the people on the server, so nothing gets pushed out of sight when
  many people are in voice.
- **A tidier bar at the bottom of the sidebar.** Settings is now in the menu that opens from
  your name, with your status. The arrows for choosing a microphone or output appear when you
  point at the mute and deafen buttons, and right-clicking either button opens the same menu.
  An admin's count of people waiting for approval shows on their picture.
- **Event times are shown in 24-hour time**, and picked with Zoia's own time list.

### Good to know

- Every notification is on to begin with. Turn off what you don't want in
  **Settings › Notifications**.
- Direct messages are private from other people on the server, admins and bots, but are kept on
  the server like any other chat.
- A repeating event starts over each time: people answer again for every occurrence.

summary: Edit your messages, silence a chat, and Zumbido waits exactly as long as a sound.
---

### New

- **Edit your messages.** Hover one of yours and pick the pencil, or press ↑ in an empty
  message box to edit your last one. Enter saves, Esc cancels. Everyone sees the change at
  once, marked **(edited)**. Only the author can edit a message.
- **Silence a chat.** Right-click a chat to silence it on this computer. It is dimmed, with
  a crossed-out bell; it counts only the messages that @mention you, and plays no sound.
  Right-click again to bring it back.

### Improved

- **Zumbido** is the soundboard's new name.
- After playing a sound, Zumbido waits exactly as long as the sound is heard, no more and
  no less, instead of a fixed time. Silence at the start and end of a sound file is
  skipped, and the bar runs smoothly over the wait.

### Fixed

- A new name now shows for everyone at once: in the People tab, in the chat and in every
  channel.
- Newly approved people appear in the People tab right away, as do role and picture
  changes.
- A chat's unread count no longer stays behind after its messages are deleted, for example
  by a bot tidying up.

### Good to know

- An addon may edit its own bots' messages, never anyone else's.

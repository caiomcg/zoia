---
summary: Smoother, steadier broadcasts on AMD Radeon graphics cards, with a bitrate that adapts to your connection.
---

### New

- **AMD hardware encoding on the room connection (Windows).** Broadcasts from a Radeon are
  now encoded by the graphics card and sent the same way NVIDIA's are: lost packets are
  resent, viewers who join or lose the picture get a fresh one straight away, and sound and
  picture stay in sync. If the card's encoder cannot start, sharing falls back to the previous
  way of sending, so no Radeon does worse than before.
- **Adaptive bitrate (Windows, NVIDIA and AMD).** While you broadcast, Zoia watches what
  your connection can carry and lowers the bitrate when it shrinks, then climbs back toward
  your quality preset when it recovers. On a slower uplink this means a softer picture for a
  moment instead of stutter and broken frames for viewers.

### Improved

- **Shared audio sounds like it should.** Game and app audio is sent in stereo at music
  quality, without the voice-style silence suppression that could be heard in quiet passages.
- **No more crackling or holes in shared audio on a Radeon.** Small differences between the
  sound device's clock and Zoia's are now absorbed smoothly, and short gaps in the capture
  are filled with silence instead of breaking up the sound.
- **The share picker opens instantly.** The list of windows and screens appears right away
  and is always current; window pictures fill in as they arrive.
- **Accurate broadcast stats.** The stats card names the encoder actually in use, and on the
  ffmpeg route shows the bitrate and frame rate really being sent rather than the preset's.

### Fixed

- Zoia no longer crashes on quit on some AMD Radeon cards (Windows).
- On the ffmpeg route, lost video packets are now resent, so viewers no longer sit on a
  broken picture until the next keyframe.
- On the ffmpeg route, sound no longer runs slightly ahead of the picture.
- On the ffmpeg route, a broadcast that failed to connect at start is now retried
  automatically instead of needing you to try again.
- A still screen no longer uses your full upload bandwidth on an AMD broadcast.
- An error while reporting a broadcast failure no longer closes the app.

### Good to know

- Intel graphics still broadcast through the ffmpeg route.
- The bitrate never goes above your quality preset; it only comes down when your connection
  needs it to.

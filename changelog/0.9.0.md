---
summary: Voice chat in every channel, with noise suppression and shortcuts, plus statuses and a People tab.
---

### New

- **Voice chat in every channel.** Joining a channel puts you in its voice, and leaving it takes
  you out; there is no button. Everyone in the channel hears everyone else. The bar above your
  card says you are connected, and holds the camera, screen and leave buttons.
- **Mute and deafen** from the microphone and headset buttons beside your card. Deafen silences
  every sound in Zoia. A short tone confirms each change. Your mute is remembered when you
  restart the app.
- **Settings → Voice**: choose your microphone, watch its level as you speak, and set the input
  sensitivity yourself or leave it on **Automatic** to follow the noise around you. You can
  also choose **Push to talk** instead of voice activity.
- **Noise suppression**, with three levels in Settings → Voice: **Off**, **Standard** for
  steady noise like a fan, and **Enhanced** for static, keyboards, clicks and voices in the room.
  Echo cancellation and automatic gain can be switched there too.
- **Shortcuts for push to talk, mute and deafen**, in Settings → Voice. Use keys or a mouse
  button. They work while Zoia is in the background.
- **Each person's voice volume.** Right-click someone in the channel to set how loud you hear
  them, up to twice as loud. Zoia remembers it for each person.
- **See who is talking and listening.** A green ring shows around whoever is speaking, yours as
  soon as your voice is being sent. A crossed-out microphone means someone is muted, and a
  crossed-out headset means they are not listening.
- **Statuses.** Click your card at the bottom of the sidebar to be online, busy, away or
  invisible. If you are online, you are shown as away after 5 minutes without using the
  computer, or as soon as the screen locks. Invisible looks offline to everyone else.
- **People tab.** The top of the sidebar switches between **Channels** and **People**. People
  lists everyone online with their status, then everyone offline.
- **Emoji button** beside the message box, with the ones you used last first. Its last
  button opens the system's emoji picker.
- **Admins: manage people from the People tab.** Right-click someone to make a reset code,
  disable or enable their account, or (super admins) make them an admin or remove that.

### Improved

- **A LIVE tag** on someone's picture while they share, instead of the green ring.
- Joining a channel keeps you where you were, rather than switching to the broadcast.
- The sidebar is a little wider, and your card at the bottom shows your name and status
  clearly.

### Good to know

- **Enhanced noise suppression is on by default**, and so is automatic input sensitivity.
- **The camera sends a picture only.** Your voice goes out through voice chat, so the camera
  no longer has a microphone of its own.
- **Talking counts as being active.** Sitting silently in a channel with nobody sharing or
  watching still disconnects you after ten minutes.
- **macOS:** shortcuts need the Accessibility permission. Without it, Settings → Voice says
  shortcuts are not available.
- **Android** does not play voice chat yet.

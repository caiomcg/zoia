### New

- **Profile pictures** (Settings › Profile). Click the circle to choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP
  picture, then drag and zoom it to frame it, with a preview at the sizes Zoia shows it.
  Everyone in the room sees it next to your name.
- **Find broadcasts in the channel list.** Hover someone who is sharing to see their
  broadcast playing beside the list, and click them, or the picture, to watch. Nothing sits
  over the stage any more outside fullscreen, so the mosaic's tiles and their controls are
  never covered.
- **Small windows in the spotlight.** The eye on a hover picture adds that broadcast as a
  small window at the bottom of the stage, with its sound. Click a small window to swap it
  with the large one; the arrow below them tucks them away and brings them back.

### Improved

- **The spotlight and the mosaic show the same broadcasts.** Switching layout only changes
  how they are laid out: the mosaic shows each one as a tile, the spotlight shows the first
  one large and the rest as small windows.
- **You can see who you are watching in the channel list:** their picture's ring turns from
  green to blue.

### Good to know

- **Each name can only be used by one person on a server.** Choosing a name someone else has
  is refused, however it is written. Where two people already shared a name, whoever had it
  longest kept it, and the other now has a number after it, such as "Alex 2".
- **Listen only is gone.** To hear a broadcast, watch it, in either layout or as a small
  window.
- In fullscreen, the other broadcasts are still in the strip over the picture; click one to
  switch to it.

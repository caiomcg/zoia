---
summary: Choose where voice plays, switch devices from the sidebar, and keep talking when a headset is unplugged.
---

### New

- **Choose your output device.** Settings › Voice has a new Output section where you pick
  where voices, streams and sounds play, with a **Test sound** button to check it.
- **Switch devices from the sidebar.** The small arrows beside the mute and deafen buttons open
  a menu of your microphones and outputs, so you can switch without opening Settings.

### Fixed

- **Unplugging a headset no longer cuts you off.** Before, unplugging the headset you were
  talking on, or plugging it back in, could leave you unheard and hearing nobody. Now Zoia
  moves to the system default while it is gone and goes back to it as soon as it returns,
  for both the microphone and the output.
- **Joining voice with your chosen microphone unplugged** no longer fails: Zoia uses the system
  default until that microphone is back.

### Improved

- **"System default" says which device it is**, for example "System default (Headset)", in
  the sidebar menus and in Settings › Voice. The message when your microphone disconnects names
  the device Zoia moved to.
- Updated to the latest Electron 44 release, with Chromium's security fixes.
- **Android:** voice, chat, profile pictures, audio settings, the admin panel and in-app updates
  in the Android app.

### Good to know

- Your chosen microphone and output are remembered even while they are unplugged. Pick
  "System default" if you'd rather always follow Windows' or macOS's choice.

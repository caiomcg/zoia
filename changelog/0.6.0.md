### New

- **Zoia for Android (viewer).** A native app for Android 10 and later that joins your Zoia
  server with the same `zoia-invite.json` the desktop app uses: give the device a name, tap
  **Importar zoia-invite.json** and pick the file. It watches broadcasts; it does not
  broadcast.
- **Channels on Android.** See every channel and who is in it, switch between them, and
  create a new one from the channel list.
- **Pick what you hear.** When several people are live, tap the speaker on a stream to hear
  that one.
- **Keep watching outside the app.** Leaving the app shrinks the broadcast into a
  picture-in-picture window, and the sound keeps playing with the screen off, shown as a
  notification while it does.
- **Manage the device from the app.** Change the name others see, or unpair the device to
  use a different invite.

### Good to know

- The app's screens are in Portuguese for now.

### Improved

- **The small windows can sit on any edge of the stage.** Drag them up, down, left or right
  and they snap to that side, so they stop covering the picture underneath. Along the top or
  bottom they stay in a row; on the left or right they stack. The arrow that hides them
  stays against the same edge.
- **They turn as you drag.** As soon as the pointer reaches another side, the windows line
  up for that side. Letting go parks them there.

### Good to know

- A click still watches, hides, or changes volume. You have to drag them to move the strip.
- The side you last chose is remembered on this computer, including in fullscreen.

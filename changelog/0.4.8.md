---
summary: Join a channel when you click it, leave with one button, and dock the small broadcast windows on any edge.
---

### New

- **Zoia no longer joins a channel on its own at launch.** It lists the channels and who is
  in them, and connects only when you click one. Until then, the stage asks you to pick a
  channel.
- **A button to leave the channel** sits beside Settings, at the bottom of the sidebar, while
  you are in one. Leaving stops anything you are sharing and returns you to the channel list.
- **About credits Stefano and Yure** in **Settings → About**.

### Improved

- **The small windows can sit on any edge of the stage.** Drag them up, down, left or right
  and they snap to that side, so they stop covering the picture underneath. Along the top or
  bottom they stay in a row; on the left or right they stack. The arrow that hides them
  stays against the same edge.
- **They turn as you drag.** As soon as the pointer reaches another side, the windows line
  up for that side. Letting go parks them there.

### Fixed

- **Clicking a hover preview no longer drops the broadcast you were watching.** It swaps
  into the large view, the same as clicking a small window on the stage. The previous one
  stays as a small window.
- **Out of every channel, the sidebar shows who is in each one** again, instead of the people
  from the last channel you left.
- **A channel that cannot be joined** shows an error and leaves you out of every channel,
  instead of dropping you into the first one.

### Good to know

- A click still watches, hides, or changes volume. You have to drag them to move the strip.
- The side you last chose is remembered on this computer, including in fullscreen.

---
summary: Drag people between channels, and a tidier Accounts page for admins.
---

### New

- **Drag to move.** Drag yourself onto another channel in the sidebar to switch to it. Admins
  can also drag someone else onto a channel to bring them there. Their app switches on its
  own and tells them who moved them. If they were sharing, sharing stops first.
- **Anderson** joins the credits in **Settings → About** for the Android app.

### Improved

- **Settings → Accounts** shows everyone with their picture, as in a channel. Each row has
  small icon buttons to reset a password, make or remove an admin, and revoke or restore
  access. Hover over a button to see what it does.
- **Disable** is now called **Revoke access**. It still signs every machine of that person
  out at once.

### Good to know

- Moving someone only works if they are on this version or newer. An older app stays where
  it is.

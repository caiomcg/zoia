---
summary: Blur or replace your camera's background, birthdays on the calendar with a party, and smoother hardware broadcasts.
---

### New

- **Blur or replace what is behind you on camera.** The camera dialog has a **Background**
  choice: none, a light or strong blur, one of four built-in backgrounds (Dusk, Ocean,
  Forest, Studio) or an image of your own. The preview shows it as viewers will see it, and
  the choice is remembered on this computer.
- **The live card holds everything you share.** While you share, the card in the sidebar
  shows how long you have been live and who is watching, with a row for the screen and one
  for the camera. From the camera's row you can change the background or switch cameras
  without turning it off, and stop either share on its own, or both with **Stop all**.
- **Birthdays.** Zoia now asks for your birthday — day and month, no year — at sign-up, or
  after you sign in if you have not given one yet. You can change it in **Settings › Profile**.
  On the day it goes on everyone's calendar as an all-day event, and Zoia celebrates with
  confetti, a sound and a notification. The person whose birthday it is gets a "Happy
  birthday" of their own.

### Fixed

- **Hardware-encoded broadcasts no longer hitch behind a game on a high-refresh monitor
  (Windows).** Sharing with an NVIDIA or AMD encoder from a 120 or 144 Hz screen while a game
  ran could stutter. Zoia now captures every refresh and keeps its capture ahead of the game
  on the graphics card.
- **Plugging in a headset mid-call no longer moves your call to it.** When the system makes a
  newly plugged-in device the default, Zoia keeps using the microphone and speakers you were
  on. Pick the new device in **Settings › Voice** or the quick device menu if you want it;
  picking "System default" follows the system again.

### Good to know

- Background effects run on your computer and use its graphics card. On a slower machine
  that is also sharing a screen they can cost some frame rate, which is why they start off.
  On a computer without WebGL2 the camera dialog says they are unavailable.
- An image you upload as a background stays on this computer and is never sent to the
  server; only your camera's picture goes out.
- Until you give your birthday, Zoia asks each time you sign in. **Later** closes the prompt
  until the next time.
- Birthdays start and end at midnight in the server's time zone, the same for everyone.
  Birthday notifications are on by default and can be turned off like the others; turning
  off the in-app one also turns off the confetti.

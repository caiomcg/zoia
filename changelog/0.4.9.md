---
summary: Smoother, in-sync game sharing on AMD and Intel graphics, with a preview of your own broadcast.
---

### Improved

- **Sharing on AMD and Intel graphics is smoother.** Games no longer hitch on high
  refresh-rate monitors: a 90 fps+ game on a 165 Hz screen now goes out at an even 60 fps
  instead of a stuttering 55.
- **Picture and sound stay in sync** for the whole broadcast on AMD and Intel graphics,
  instead of drifting apart over time.
- **Resizing a shared window no longer cuts it off.** On AMD and Intel graphics, a window
  made bigger or smaller while you share it is shown whole, with bars at the sides if needed,
  instead of only its top-left corner.

### New

- **See your own broadcast on AMD and Intel graphics.** The stage now shows what viewers
  receive, and the preview button in the stage footer hides or shows it, as on NVIDIA.

### Fixed

- **Broadcasts on AMD graphics no longer cut out** when the picture stays still for a while.
- **Sharing a whole screen** uses the graphics card on AMD and Intel too, at the screen's full
  resolution, and shares the screen you picked on a computer with more than one.
- **Starting a broadcast right after stopping one** no longer fails; Zoia tries again on its
  own.
- **Switching what you share while it is still starting**, such as the League client handing
  over to the game, no longer ends the broadcast or drops it to the slower, processor-based
  sharing.
- **The yellow border Windows draws around a shared window** is turned off more reliably
  when you have chosen to hide it.

### Good to know

- These changes are for Windows. NVIDIA graphics cards share the same way as before.
- On Windows 10 the yellow capture border cannot be turned off.

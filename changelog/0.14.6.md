---
summary: Zoia opens again on Windows PCs with Smart App Control on.
---

### Fixed

- **Zoia closing at startup on some Windows PCs.** With Smart App Control on, Windows refused
  one of Zoia's components and Zoia closed at once with "An Application Control policy has
  blocked this file". It now opens without that component.

### Good to know

- On those PCs, sharing a window still works, but some things that rely on the blocked
  component are off: a shared window carries no sound from its application, its name in the
  share doesn't follow the window's title, and closing the shared window doesn't end the
  share by itself. The window list may also include a few windows that can't be shared.
- Windows only.

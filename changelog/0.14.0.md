---
summary: Stop sharing with one click, an away channel, sounds when people watch you, and Zoia's own colours.
---

### New

- **Stop sharing, right where you can see it.** While you share your screen, a red card above
  the share buttons shows what you're sharing, for how long and how many are watching, with
  **Stop sharing** and **Switch**. The "You're live" screen has the same two buttons.
- **An away channel.** An admin can make one channel the away channel in Settings → Server.
  In it nobody talks or shares: you're muted, you hear no one, and the share buttons are off.
  It shows a moon in the channel list, and people there are disconnected after an hour of
  inactivity instead of the usual time.
- **Hear who's watching.** A soft sound plays when someone starts or stops watching your
  stream. Each has its own switch and volume in Settings → Sounds.
- **Mentions and commands stand out as you type.** In the message box, a person you picked
  from the suggestions and a bot `!command` get the same accent they have once sent.
- **Bots have a robot badge**, where an admin's crown sits, everywhere they appear. The super
  admin can give each bot a picture in Settings → Addons.

### Improved

- **Zoia's own colours.** The app takes its blue from the Zoia logo, with its own greys,
  greens and reds.
- **Settings are grouped into titled cards**, the way Voice already was, with switches
  instead of checkboxes, and text that was only in English is now translated.
- The settings button is a gear.

### Fixed

- An update no longer fails when Zoia was sharing at the moment you updated, or when an
  earlier Zoia was still closing.
- On Windows installs in Program Files, Zoia no longer reopens as administrator after an
  update, which made sharing a window fail until it was restarted.
- With the sidebar collapsed, a bot's row no longer looks broken.
- The away-channel list in Settings is dark, like the rest of the app.

### Good to know

- The away channel needs the latest Zoia to keep people muted there.
- A person is highlighted in the message box once picked from the suggestions: only picked
  people are notified.

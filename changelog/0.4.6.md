### Fixed

- **Sharing with hardware acceleration works again** on Windows with an NVIDIA graphics card.
  Since 0.4.1 the broadcast could end as soon as it started, or viewers saw only a black
  picture, and stopping the share could freeze Zoia.
- **The broadcast follows the shared window** when it is resized, made fullscreen, or put back
  in a window.

---
summary: A soundboard in voice, with your own pad of sounds, plus crowns in the People tab.
---

### New

- **Soundboard.** While you are in voice, the music-note button under **Voice connected**
  opens it. Search Myinstants for a sound and click it to play it for everyone in your
  channel. You hear it too.
- **Your pad.** Add up to 10 sounds to your pad with **+**, then play them with one click.
  Hover a pad to preview it (only you hear it) or edit it. Right-clicking a pad also edits it.
  You can rename it, emoji included, give it an emoji of its own, and set a keyboard or mouse
  shortcut that works while Zoia is in the background.
- **Recent sounds.** The last 4 sounds you played that are not on your pad, ready to play again
  or add. Remove any of them with **×**.
- **Sound effects volume.** Set it from the soundboard or in **Settings → Sounds**, or mute
  every sound effect. The mouse wheel moves the slider.
- **Mute one person's sound effects.** Right-click them in your channel. Their voice is
  unaffected.
- **Crowns in the People tab**, as in the channel list: silver for an admin, gold for the
  super admin.

### Improved

- Screen, camera and the soundboard are now a row of buttons under **Voice connected**. Leave
  stays beside it.
- **Voice connected** shows the state of your connection: hover it for ping and, while you
  share, how your broadcast is doing. It turns red if the connection fails. The dot in the
  top bar is gone.

### Fixed

- Someone sharing only their camera now shows the LIVE tag.
- An admin's picture under a channel no longer sits lower than everyone else's.
- Release notes opened from Settings → About are no longer cut off in a large window.

### Good to know

- **The name you give a pad is what others see** beside your name while it plays, its emoji
  first.
- You can play a sound only with your microphone on, and once every two seconds. Every sound
  stops after ten seconds.
- Sounds come from Myinstants, so each sound is downloaded from there by everyone in the
  channel who hears it.
- **macOS:** soundboard shortcuts need the Accessibility permission, as voice's do.
- **Android** does not play soundboard sounds yet.

---
summary: Delete chat messages, and the Broadcast tab opens as soon as you join a channel.
---

### New

- **Delete a message.** Hover one of your messages and click the bin beside **Reply**. Admins
  can delete anyone's. It disappears for everyone at once, a photo or video with it, and a
  reply to it shows "Message deleted" instead of quoting it.

### Improved

- **Joining a channel opens the Broadcast tab**, whether or not anyone is live yet, with the
  chat a click away. Choosing someone's stream in the channel list, or in its hover preview,
  brings you to the Broadcast tab with their screen large, from the chat too.
- **Disconnecting only takes someone out of their channel.** When an admin disconnects you, or
  you sit in a channel for ten minutes neither sharing nor watching, you leave the channel and
  Zoia says why. The chats stay open, and you can join a channel again at once.
- **Collapsed sidebar:** only your picture shows at the bottom; click it to open the sidebar,
  where leaving and settings are. Sign-ups waiting for an admin show as a dot on it.
- The tab bar above the chat stays put when you switch tabs, and lines up with the first
  channel in the sidebar; the bar with your name lines up with the message box.
- Confirming a deletion uses Zoia's own dialog.
- **Watch while chatting** is gone: each broadcast's own picture-in-picture button does the
  same.

### Fixed

- The message box no longer stops responding to clicks after you confirm a deletion.
- Chat no longer says it is unavailable for a while after the server restarts.
- The Broadcast tab no longer shows a pulsing green dot while you watch someone.

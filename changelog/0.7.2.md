### Improved

- **Moves show at once.** When an admin drags someone onto a channel, they appear there right
  away instead of a couple of seconds later.
- **No pop-up when you are moved.** Your app just switches channel, without the yellow
  banner.
- **Equals move themselves.** Admins can drag people below them, and the super admin can drag
  admins too, but nobody can drag someone of the same rank. Someone who has only just joined
  can be dragged once their role shows.

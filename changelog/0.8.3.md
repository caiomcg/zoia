---
summary: A full-window photo viewer with zoom and download, clickable links, and a quick way back to the latest message.
---

### New

- **Photo viewer.** Click a photo in a chat to open it on the whole window. Click to zoom in
  where you clicked and click again to fit it back; while zoomed, drag to move around. The
  mouse wheel zooms too. Press Escape or click beside the photo to close it.
- **Download photos and videos** from the viewer's **Download** button, which asks where to
  save the file.
- **Videos open larger** from the button in their corner when you hover them.
- **Links in messages are clickable** and open in your browser.
- **Jump to latest.** Scroll far back in a chat and a button appears above the message box to
  take you straight to the newest messages.

### Fixed

- Photos in a chat fit their space instead of being cut off.

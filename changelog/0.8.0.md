summary: Text chats beside the channels, with photos, replies and @mentions, and a tidier sidebar.
---

### New

- **Text chats.** Under the channels in the sidebar is a list of chats, which everyone on the
  server can read and write in, whichever channel they are in. Click one to open it in the main
  area. Unread messages are counted beside each chat, a "New messages" line marks where you
  left off, and "Seen by" under the last message says who has read it.
- **Photos and videos in chats**, up to 10 MB each: use the clip beside the send button, paste
  one, or drop it on the chat. Whatever you type is sent as its caption. Click a photo to see it
  large.
- **Reply to a message.** Hover it and click **Reply**. The message box shows what you are
  answering, and the reply carries a quote of it; click the quote to jump back to the original.
- **@mention people.** Type `@` and pick someone from the list that appears, with the arrow keys
  and Enter or Tab. Mentions are highlighted, and a message that mentions you stands out.
- **Watch while chatting.** While someone is live, tabs above the main area switch between the
  broadcast and the chat, and **Watch while chatting** keeps the broadcast in a small window on
  top while the chat fills the main area.
- **Emoji in channel and chat names.** When naming one, the 🙂 button beside the name offers
  common emoji, and its last cell opens the system's emoji picker for the rest.
- **Admins: disconnect someone.** Right-click someone in the channel list and choose
  **Disconnect**. Their app leaves its channel and the chat and shows a Disconnected screen
  until they press **Reconnect**. It is not a ban.
- **Admins: Settings → Server** sets how many channels and chats there may be.

### Improved

- **A tidier sidebar.** The **Channels** and **Chats** headings fold their lists, and the app
  remembers which are folded. A folded list still shows the channel you are in, the open chat
  and any chat with unread messages. Admins add a channel or chat with the **+** that appears
  when hovering a heading.
- **Channel names are easier to read**, with a screen icon beside each, and a count only when
  someone is in the channel. Under a channel you are not in, five faces show, then **+N** for
  the rest.
- **Open and close the sidebar from its edge.** Drag the line between the main area and the
  sidebar, or double-click it. The arrow button is gone.
- A collapsed sidebar shows a channel's emoji in its badge when its name starts with one.

### Good to know

- **Ten minutes in a channel without sharing or watching anyone disconnects you**, so the list
  shows who is really there. Press **Reconnect** to come back. Chatting does not count as
  watching, so leave the channel if you only want the chat.
- **Chat photos and videos are kept up to a total size on the server.** When it is reached, the
  oldest are removed and their messages say the photo or video is no longer available.
- **Android:** the app has no chat. It now tells the server which broadcasts it shows, so
  watching from a tablet does not count as idle, and broadcasters see an eye for Android
  viewers too. An older Android app is disconnected after ten minutes; update it.
